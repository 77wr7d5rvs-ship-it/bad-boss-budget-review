03 · STUDENT WORKING NOTES

My written judgement

I have written this as my submission: clear assumptions, a correction where the evidence is weak, and a recommendation that management can actually control.

Assumption review

I have not treated targets as sales. The 26 events are a planning placeholder, and placeholders do not reserve trainers, rooms or client dates. I therefore used the supported 2025 revenue as my cautious Part 1 base.

My Part 2 decision

I would approve a phased autumn campaign, but only after Operations books delivery dates and Finance releases the spend in stages. Ella should be treated as productive from August, not from her May start date.

Remaining risks

The conversion rate is untested, external trainer availability is not guaranteed, and the July instructor rate rises by 10%. These risks need a monthly reforecast instead of optimistic smoothing.
