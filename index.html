Recovery decisions

What creates the €110,000 extra sales?

Autumn leadership cohorts

2 cohorts × 10 seats × €3,000 = €60,000. Ella sells with the Sales Director; senior trainers deliver in September and October. Lead time: 8 weeks.

Manager workshops

4 workshops × 10 seats × €1,500 × 50% occupancy = €30,000. Add 2 executive clinics × 5 seats × €2,000 = €20,000; together this completes the €110,000 bridge. Marketing generates qualified leads; delivery uses existing trainer days in August–November.

Cost logic

Direct delivery cost is budgeted at 59.5% of incremental sales, or €65,450: 44.55% rate-adjusted delivery plus 14.95% venue, materials and assistant capacity. Commercial/capacity spend is €30,000.

The resulting bridge is phased Aug–Dec: €20k executive clinics in August, €30k leadership cohort delivery in September, €30k leadership/workshop delivery in October, €20k workshops in November and €10k workshops in December. Marketing preparation begins in June as a Part 2 decision, using the existing Apr–Aug small-campaign window; the larger September–November campaign supports the autumn pipeline. The six-to-eight-week corporate lead time is met before September delivery; delivery begins only after bookings. No January–July recovery revenue is assumed before Ella’s ramp, lead qualification and delivery bookings.
