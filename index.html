BOARD RECOMMENDATION

Reject the €627k profit claim.
Approve a gated recovery plan instead.

1. Protect the base

Keep the corrected budget visible; do not treat unbooked sales as delivered revenue.

2. Book before selling

Prioritise program dates and instructor capacity six to eight weeks ahead.

3. Fund proven acquisition

Release campaign spend only against qualified leads and conversion evidence.

4. Review monthly

Track revenue, direct costs, delivery capacity, staffing and marketing conversion.
