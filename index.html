03 · WRITTEN TASK

My written judgement

Assumption review

I have not treated targets as sales. The 26 events are a planning placeholder, so they do not reserve trainers, rooms or client dates. I used the supported 2025 revenue as the cautious base.

Management decision

I would approve a phased autumn campaign only after Operations books delivery dates and Finance releases spend in stages. Ella should be treated as productive from August.

Remaining risks

Conversion is untested, external trainer availability is not guaranteed, and instructor rates rise by 10% from July. I would reforecast these risks monthly.

05 · BOARD RECOMMENDATION

Approve a controlled recovery plan

The board should not approve the headline profit as if it were already earned. It should approve a controlled route to growth, with clear gates and evidence at each review.

01 · Protect the baseUse the corrected case as the control base and keep the original draft as an upside target.
02 · Book before sellingSecure client dates, trainers and rooms six to eight weeks ahead of delivery.
03 · Fund evidenceRelease campaign spend only against qualified leads, conversion evidence and available capacity.
04 · Review monthlyTrack revenue, margin, utilisation, staffing, trainer rates and marketing performance together.
